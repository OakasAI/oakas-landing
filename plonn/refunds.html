Billing & refunds

Apple handles PLONN’s App Store subscription payments. Cancellation and refunds are separate actions.

Effective: October 26, 2026 · Version: 2026-10-26

Request a refund

Open Apple’s refund page using the Apple Account on your receipt. Choose Request a refund and a reason, then select the purchase and submit the request. Apple reviews eligibility; a request does not guarantee a refund. Applicable consumer rights still apply.

Stop renewal

Use Manage Apple subscriptions to cancel renewal or change your plan. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Cancelling does not automatically refund a previous charge. Deleting PLONN or your account does not cancel the subscription.

Restore access

If you already purchased Pro, sign in to the PLONN account used for that purchase and choose Restore purchases on the Pro screen. Use the Apple purchase account associated with the subscription. Restore does not buy a new subscription.

Missing charges or access

A pending charge may not be available for a refund request yet. Check your Apple receipt and purchase account, or contact Apple Support about billing. For PLONN access issues, contact jake@oakas.ai without sending passwords or payment-card information. TestFlight sandbox purchases do not charge real money.
