Contact OAKAS.

For PLONN support, email support@oakas.ai. Put PLONN in the subject.

For a bug report, include the app version/build, iOS version, screen, steps and visible error. For research issues, include the sport, event or player, market and displayed data timestamp. Remove personal information before attaching a screenshot.

Never send your password, authentication or recovery links, login tokens, payment information or a full device data export. Confirmation and recovery messages come from noreply@plonn-mail.oakas.ai; use the support address above for help. Privacy questions use the contact on the privacy page.

Account and deletion.

How do I sign in?

Open Settings → Account & sync, choose Sign in and enter your email and password. For a new account, choose Sign up, add a display name and a password of at least eight characters, then check your email for confirmation. An optional referral code is checked after confirmation.

Open a confirmation email on the same device and in the app that started signup. Once confirmation succeeds, the app may sign you in; otherwise return to Account & sync and sign in with your email and password. Email/password is the supported account method. Apple, Google and passwordless sign-in are not offered in this build.

How do I reset my password?

Enter your account email in Settings → Account & sync, then tap Forgot password?. Open the recovery email link on the same device using the app that requested it. When PLONN presents the recovery form, enter and confirm a new password of at least eight characters.

If the email is missing, check spam and the address you entered. If a link has expired or was already used, request a new one. Do not forward a recovery link or share it with support. The OAKAS website does not have a PLONN sign-in or password-reset form.

Why does an email link open the wrong app or fail to return?

Install the PLONN build you intend to use before requesting the email. Request a new link from that installed app and open it on the same device. Production and development preview builds use separate return links; a link requested from one does not sign in the other.

If it still fails, report the installed app version/build and whether you requested confirmation or recovery. Do not send the link itself. You can sign in with your email and password after account confirmation.

How do I delete my account?
  1. Sign in, then open Settings → Account & sync.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm Delete permanently.
  3. Wait for the app’s account-deleted confirmation. If a model job is running, let it finish and retry. Check your connection and sign-in if the request fails.

The app requests removal of hosted model runs, queued jobs and fitted artifacts before deleting the Supabase account and associated account-owned records. A service failure can prevent completion. If hosted removal succeeds but account deletion fails, your account can still exist; retry the account action.

Device copies are separate: clear the watchlist in Settings → Clear saved watchlist and the calculator draft through Selection calculator → Clear draft. Signing out or uninstalling does not delete your account. Some usage-limit, provider-credit, backup, log and delivery records can remain after deletion; see storage and retention.

What if I cannot sign in to delete my account?

Try password recovery first. If recovery or the in-app deletion action still fails, contact OAKAS support with PLONN in the subject. Do not send passwords or recovery links. Help involving account records requires verification that you own the account.

Saved research.

Why does my watchlist differ between devices?

The watchlist stays on each device by default. To copy it to your account, use Account & sync → Copy device watchlist to cloud, then Refresh cloud watchlist to view cloud records. A cloud item is a saved snapshot, not a current quote. Removing it does not remove a device copy.

How do I remove a saved model or calculator draft?

In My Models, tap the model’s trash button and confirm Delete model. This removes the saved configuration and its private versions; it does not by itself remove an available fitted artifact from the research host.

In the selection calculator, tap Clear draft and confirm. If a saved-draft conflict appears, use the displayed restore or keep-current controls. Do not clear a draft unless you intend to discard it.

Odds, value and edge.

Why are odds unavailable?

Sign in for sportsbook odds, check your connection and read the app’s status. A competition or player-prop feed may be unsupported, the provider may be unavailable or the refresh allowance may have been reached. If asked to refresh the slate, select a listed matchup afterward. If asked to wait, follow that message; repeated refreshes do not bypass limits.

Odds are snapshots and can change. A stored selection may show an old price; it is not necessarily usable now.

What does Explore research show?

Explore compares individual selections before offering combinations. Where data permits, a research card shows the best usable sportsbook price, the probability implied by that price, recent evidence, its source and freshness, and the limits of the comparison. One available sportsbook is limited coverage, not proof of the best price everywhere.

Recent player results describe a historical sample. They are not a win probability, and a team average is not an individual player projection. Combining selections adds uncertainty; same-game combinations require an actual sportsbook quote.

Why is estimated edge unavailable?

Estimated edge needs both a current matching validated model probability and a usable price for the exact event, start, subject, market, side and line. It is the model probability minus the price-implied probability, expressed in percentage points. It can be positive, zero or negative.

The current odds feed does not supply validated live model probabilities, so a usable best price can appear while edge remains unavailable. Historical hit rates, saved configurations and AI text do not fill that gap. Stale or conflicting quotes, unsupported settlement rules, integer thresholds that can push and ambiguous tie/overtime rules can also prevent edge.

Price-implied probability includes sportsbook margin. Estimated edge is uncertain; it does not guarantee a winner or a return.

Models and AI chat.

What does a model result mean?

Coverage depends on the sport, target, market, season and available observations. Training and validation can be unavailable with insufficient data. An available fitted artifact may use a frozen target snapshot rather than current inputs.

Historical validation does not establish future performance or profitability. Review the data source, target date, sample size and limitations shown in the app. Experimental results are research, not approved betting advice.

Why is a model run queued, busy or unavailable?

The service has shared capacity and account limits. Let an accepted request finish and check its status instead of repeatedly starting new runs. A quota or capacity message is not a failed payment. Preserve the displayed reason if you need help.

How do AI chat and matchup Insights work?

The next TestFlight build is intended to enable phone AI chat and AI-generated matchup Insights. Build 2 has these phone AI features disabled. Sign in and review the account-specific consent prompt before either feature sends a request. You can decline; no AI request should be sent without that consent.

Chat uses your question and limited recent context. Matchup Insights interprets selected evidence for the two teams without sending chat history. Both use OAKAS’s self-hosted Gemma4 service. Read the AI data disclosure before using them. Generated explanations can be incorrect and do not establish a win probability or betting edge.

Why is AI unavailable, busy or at its limit?

This temporary beta depends on the OAKAS Mac remaining awake and connected, and its gateway and encrypted connection staying active. It can be unavailable without notice.

Chat and matchup Insights share a limit of five admitted attempts per account and twenty across the service per UTC day, with one inference at a time. Admitted failures can consume an attempt. A new UTC day begins a new daily allowance, but unresolved work can keep the service busy. Repeated requests do not bypass these limits. If unavailable, review the research evidence that remains available and try later.

Are paid tiers available?

Purchases and subscriptions are disabled in this build. No payment is required by the account confirmation or password recovery flow. Any future purchase must show the applicable App Store product and terms before payment.
