Your data in PLONN.

PLONN is a sports research app operated by OAKAS AI LLC. This page explains what stays on your device, what account and research services receive, and what happens when you remove data.

What this page covers.

This page covers PLONN’s iOS app and its account, odds and model research services. The OAKAS website and business assistant policy covers the wider website and business assistant. Its SMS, WhatsApp, Stripe and website advertising disclosures do not describe PLONN’s native app.

The current native app contains no advertising or analytics SDK. These PLONN pages do not add scripts, tracking pixels, forms, cookies or remote fonts. Website hosting receives normal request information such as IP address and browser metadata. The wider OAKAS website uses Meta Pixel; opening other OAKAS pages may involve that website’s tracking.

Where requests go.

Supabase provides account authentication and database services. Account credentials go to its authentication service; cloud selections and private configurations go to its database. PLONN’s research service uses Supabase to verify account access.

Resend delivers Supabase’s account confirmation and recovery email. The configured sender is PLONN <noreply@plonn-mail.oakas.ai>. It is an automated sender, not the support contact.

PLONN’s research service processes model setups and stores account-scoped jobs, results and available fitted artifacts. It retrieves public historical data from ESPN, MLB Stats API and, for some NFL research, nflverse sources hosted on GitHub.

ESPN and MLB receive direct app requests for schedules, player history and images. Direct requests reveal your device’s IP address and normal request information, together with the requested sports entities. Requests made by the research service reveal the host’s network information instead.

The Odds API supplies sportsbook snapshots through PLONN’s backend. The backend sends the selected sport/event and provider request parameters. This request does not forward your PLONN email, login token or account identifier to The Odds API.

Account and hosted research connections use HTTPS. Login sessions use the iOS Keychain, and account records are accessed using authentication and owner checks. Service providers can maintain operational, delivery and security records of their own. This page does not promise immediate deletion from their logs or backups.

Purchases and paid subscriptions are disabled in the current build. PLONN does not accept or place wagers. If payment features are introduced, the policy and purchase flow will need to describe the applicable processing and terms before use.

AI chat and matchup Insights.

The next TestFlight build is intended to enable optional phone AI chat and AI-generated matchup Insights. Build 2 has these phone AI features disabled. Availability depends on your installed build and the beta service.

Before either feature sends a request, the app asks for consent for your signed-in account. Chat sends your question, up to six recent conversational turns and up to four selected research facts, including their source details. General chat can include earlier general-chat answers. Matchup Insights sends the two team names, a request for an explanation and selected matchup evidence; it does not send chat history.

Requests travel over HTTPS to PLONN’s authenticated service and through an encrypted connection to an OAKAS-operated Mac running Gemma4. Model inference runs on that Mac, not on your phone. Prompts are not sent to a third-party LLM provider. Supabase verifies account access; your account identifier is used by the gateway for usage limits, not included in the model prompt.

The chat gateway processes questions, supplied context and generated answers in memory and does not write their content to its application database. It stores separate usage records containing a hashed account identifier, UTC day, attempt identifier and capacity state. These records are account-linked, not anonymous. Finished records older than the previous UTC day are eligible for cleanup when a later request is admitted; there is no guaranteed deletion time. Unfinished reservations remain until operator recovery. Account deletion does not currently purge these chat usage records.

The gateway and chat route have access logging disabled. This does not establish a deletion period or a no-storage guarantee for the model runtime, infrastructure error logs, temporary files or backups. Do not include passwords, authentication links, tokens or sensitive personal information in questions.

AI explanations can be incorrect. Matchup Insights interprets supplied research evidence; it is not a live-data feed, a validated win probability or a guarantee of a result.

Storage and retention.

Device records remain separate from account records. Signing out, removing a cloud item or deleting your account does not clear the device watchlist, calculator draft or other local research copies.

Supabase’s account-owned records, including profiles, cloud watchlists, private models, community records, referrals and access records, are configured to be removed with the account. Separate provider-credit accounting can retain your account identifier, lifetime usage budget, credit amounts and reservation records after account deletion. These accounting records have no automatic expiry and are not removed by account deletion. These identifiers are pseudonymous and account-linked, not anonymous.

The research service makes stored run results available for seven days. Cleanup of old results and job records runs during service activity rather than at a guaranteed deletion time. That does not establish a seven-day erasure deadline for every copy. Available fitted artifacts remain until hosted account data is purged. Deleting a saved configuration in My Models does not by itself purge a fitted artifact on the research host.

Account deletion first requests removal of your hosted runs, queued jobs and fitted artifacts, then requests deletion of the Supabase account and its associated account-owned records. A running job or a service failure can block completion. If hosted removal succeeds but account deletion fails, the account can remain; retry and wait for the app’s confirmation.

Some service-limit, attempt and provider-credit records can remain after this sequence. We do not currently specify a fixed deletion period for remaining service records, infrastructure logs, backups, email delivery records or support correspondence. Account deletion must not be understood as immediate erasure of every operational record or a provider’s backup.

Your choices and deletion.

  • Use Settings → Account & sync → Copy device watchlist to cloud only when you want those selections stored with your account. Remove cloud selection removes that cloud item.
  • In My Models, tap the trash button and confirm Delete model to remove the saved model and its private configuration versions.
  • Use Settings → Clear saved watchlist for this device’s watchlist. Use Selection calculator → Clear draft and confirm to clear the calculator draft.
  • To delete your account, sign in and open Settings → Account & sync → Delete account, then confirm Delete permanently. Wait for the account-deleted confirmation. If a model job is active, let it finish and retry. A failed request is not confirmation of deletion.
  • Signing out ends your local session without deleting your account or device selections. Removing the app is not account deletion. See account recovery and deletion help.

If you cannot recover access or use the in-app deletion action, contact OAKAS using the details below. Do not send credentials or recovery links. Any request involving account records requires confirmation that the account belongs to you. Rights to access, correction or deletion can depend on where you live.

Privacy questions.

For PLONN privacy requests, contact OAKAS AI LLC at jake@oakas.ai. Identify PLONN in your subject and describe your request without including passwords, authentication links or tokens. General app help is on the support page.
